About CHEM REPORT

CHEM REPORT is an interactive academic project for chemical discovery, molecular visualisation and chemical research. It searches PubChem (PUG REST, no key needed) for compound records, optionally queries ChemSpider with your own API key, and renders structures in 3D with 3Dmol.js. SMILES strings can also be parsed and analysed locally.

Live search needs an internet connection. No chemical values are hard-coded: properties shown come from PubChem or are computed from the structure. Your ChemSpider key stays only in your browser.
